    Welcome to Speedrun
    1. Add a task with your guess, e.g. Write copy 25m
    2. The round button by the clock starts and pauses, or ⌘⇧Space from any app
    3. The circle by the task name finishes it and shows the next, or ⌘⇧↩
    4. Hover a task and click + to give it subtasks
    5. At the end of a session, save it as a template to run it again and race your best
    6. Click the clock to fold this away, drag it to move, drag its corner to resize
